# Code of Conduct

## 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## ✅ Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- **Empathy** — Listen actively, assume good intent
- **Respect** — Disagree without being disagreeable
- **Inclusivity** — Welcome newcomers, mentor patiently
- **Constructive feedback** — Specific, actionable, kind
- **Credit** — Acknowledge others' work and ideas
- **Teacher-first mindset** — Prioritize educator needs

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, flame wars, or bad-faith arguments
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sexualized language or advances
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic noise
- Gatekeeping ("real developers use X")

---

## 🎓 Community Context

**This project serves teachers.** Our community includes:
- K-12 educators (often non-technical)
- University professors & TAs
- Corporate trainers
- Students learning to code
- Open source enthusiasts

**Adjust your communication:** Avoid jargon, explain acronyms, be patient with questions.

---

## 🛡 Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying standards
- Taking fair corrective action
- Removing/editing/rejecting non-aligned content
- Communicating reasons for moderation

---

## ⚖ Enforcement Guidelines

| Level | Behavior | Consequence |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **1. Correction** | Minor violation (tone, off-topic) | Private written warning, public clarification |
| **2. Warning** | Repeated or clear violation | Temporary restriction (7 days) |
| **3. Temporary Ban** | Serious violation (harassment, spam) | 30-day ban from all community spaces |
| **4. Permanent Ban** | Severe/repeated (hate speech, doxxing) | Permanent removal |

## 📢 Reporting

**Report to:** conduct@muchhad-quiz.cc.cd or DM maintainer @shiv712

**Include:**
- Your contact info (optional)
- Names/usernames involved
- Description of incident
- Links/screenshots/context
- Desired outcome

**All reports confidential.** We investigate promptly and fairly.
